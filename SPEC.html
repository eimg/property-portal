01 Product overview

Build an English-language property marketplace for people buying, selling, or renting property in Yangon and Mandalay, Myanmar. The first version supports the web, an API, and a mobile app as three independent projects.

The coordination root contains the specification and project coordination material, not application code. app/, api/, and mobile/ are separate repositories with their own Git history, dependencies, releases, and README files.

02 Goals

  • Let visitors find and compare properties for sale or rent in Yangon and Mandalay.
  • Let property owners or authorized agents publish and manage listings.
  • Make listing price, location, transaction type, and contact path clear.
  • Provide consistent data and behavior through the API to web and mobile clients.
  • Keep the first release English-only and MMK-only.

03 First-release scope

In scope

  • Buy and Rent discovery journeys; Sell for creating and managing a sale listing.
  • Yangon and Mandalay only; English interface text only.
  • MMK listing prices only, with no foreign-currency display or conversion.
  • Responsive web client, REST API, and Expo-based mobile client.
  • Public listing search and detail pages.
  • Registration and sign-in for listing owners/agents.
  • Owner listing creation, editing, submission, and removal; administrator review and management.
  • Listing inquiries through contact information or actions provided by the owner.

Out of scope

  • Other cities or regions and translated interface catalogs.
  • Non-MMK prices, currency conversion, payments, deposits, escrow, or rental billing.
  • Map search, live chat, agent subscriptions, promoted listings, analytics dashboards, and recommendation algorithms.
  • Guaranteed transactions, ownership verification, and legal or valuation advice.

04 Users and permissions

  • Visitor: browse, search, filter, and view active listings and their public contact options.
  • Registered owner/agent: all visitor capabilities, plus create, edit, submit, and manage only their own listings.
  • Administrator: review submitted listings, publish or reject them, and remove listings that violate marketplace rules.
Authorization rule: The API is authoritative for identity, permissions, and listing state. Clients must not rely on hiding controls as an access-control measure.

05 Core user journeys

Find a property to buy or rent

  1. Choose Buy or Rent.
  2. Choose Yangon or Mandalay, with optional township and other available filters.
  3. Browse matching active listings and refine or clear filters.
  4. Open a listing to review photos, price, location, property facts, description, and contact options.
  5. Contact the owner/agent through the available contact action.

Sell or offer a rental property

  1. Register or sign in.
  2. Create a listing, selecting Sale or Rent, city, and required property information.
  3. Save a draft or submit it for administrator review.
  4. See its current status and edit it when permitted.
  5. View it publicly after approval and activation.

Moderate listings

  1. An administrator opens the review queue.
  2. The administrator reviews listing details and photos.
  3. Publish or reject the submission, giving a reason for rejection.
  4. The owner sees the resulting status and can correct and resubmit a rejected listing.

06 Listing requirements

Each listing should support:

  • A title and descriptive text.
  • Transaction type: SALE or RENT.
  • Property category, such as apartment/condo, house, land, or commercial property.
  • City (YANGON or MANDALAY) and optional township/address details.
  • Asking price as a positive whole-number MMK amount. Store an integer; do not store formatted commas or a converted value.
  • Category-appropriate facts, such as bedrooms, bathrooms, floor area, land area, and furnishing status when applicable.
  • One or more photos with an explicit primary image and stable ordering.
  • Owner/agent contact methods and listing ownership.
  • Creation and update timestamps, plus moderation/publication status.
Privacy and lifecycle: Do not require residential-only facts for land or commercial listings. Public views must not expose private account credentials or internal moderation notes. Suggested lifecycle: DRAFT → PENDING_REVIEW → ACTIVE or REJECTED. Owners may edit drafts and rejected listings and resubmit. Only active listings appear in public search. Administrators may deactivate or remove active listings. The API enforces valid transitions and permissions.

07 Functional requirements

Public discovery

  • Display a home/search entry point for Buy and Rent and a clear Sell entry point.
  • Search active listings by keyword and filter by transaction type, city, township where available, property category, and price range.
  • Provide useful empty, loading, and error states, and allow users to clear filters.
  • Paginate or progressively load results so a large inventory does not require one oversized response.
  • Sort results consistently, with newest listings available as the default ordering.

Listing details

  • Show complete public listing information and the ordered photo gallery.
  • Format prices for readability as whole MMK amounts, retaining MMK as the only currency.
  • Make city and available township information easy to find.
  • Provide an actionable contact path without exposing non-public account data.

Accounts and listing management

  • Provide registration, sign-in, sign-out, and authenticated session handling.
  • Provide an owner area for creating, editing, submitting, and reviewing that owner's listings.
  • Clearly distinguish draft, pending review, active, rejected, and removed/unavailable states.
  • Protect owner operations from changes to listings belonging to another account.

Administration

  • Provide a protected review queue for pending submissions.
  • Allow administrators to inspect the full listing and its photos, approve it, reject it with a reason, or deactivate/remove it.
  • Record who performed a moderation action and when.

English and MMK presentation

  • All product interface labels, validation messages, and system-generated messages are in English in v1.
  • Listing titles and descriptions are user-authored and displayed as entered; they are not machine-translated.
  • Show MMK or Ks consistently. Do not show $, exchange rates, or converted equivalents.
  • Accept whole-kyat amounts only; reject zero, negative, fractional, malformed, or out-of-range amounts with clear validation.

08 Technical architecture

Required technology choices for the first version:

ProjectRequired stackResponsibility
app/ (web)React, Vite, shadcn/ui initialized with preset b6sUen30yResponsive public marketplace, account/listing management, and admin interface
api/Node.js, Express, Prisma 7, SQLite for developmentREST API, validation, authentication/authorization, listing lifecycle, and persistence
mobile/React Native, ExpoMobile marketplace client using the same API and product rules

Each directory is a separate Git repository, not a package/workspace in a monorepo. Do not share a Git root, lockfile, package manifest, or local source imports across them. Shared behavior is defined by this specification and the API contract. Keep SPEC.md at the coordination root.

API baseline

  • Use versioned JSON REST endpoints under /api/v1.
  • Validate all request bodies, query parameters, and identifiers at the API boundary.
  • Return consistent JSON errors with appropriate HTTP status codes; never return stack traces or secrets to clients.
  • Enforce ownership and administrator permissions on the server.
  • Use Prisma 7 migrations/schema as the development persistence contract. SQLite is the development database; production database selection is a later deployment decision.
  • Keep credentials and secrets out of source control and never store plaintext passwords.

The precise endpoint list and deployment infrastructure are implementation decisions that must remain consistent with these product behaviors.

09 Quality requirements

  • Layouts work on common mobile and desktop viewport sizes.
  • Core flows are keyboard-operable and use semantic controls, visible focus, labels, and sufficient contrast.
  • Photos have useful alternative text where meaningful; decorative imagery is hidden from assistive technology.
  • Search and listing pages expose understandable loading, empty, validation, and failure states.
  • Check API authorization on each protected operation, including attempts to access another user's listing by changing an identifier.
  • Store and transmit prices as whole MMK values without floating-point currency arithmetic.
  • Do not seed or publish fabricated claims that a listing has been verified or guarantees a transaction.

10 Acceptance criteria

  1. A visitor can browse, search, and filter sale and rental listings in Yangon and Mandalay on web and mobile.
  2. Listing cards and details show prices only in MMK and do not offer currency conversion.
  3. A visitor can open a listing and use its available owner/agent contact method.
  4. A registered owner/agent can create a draft, submit it, and track its moderation status.
  5. An administrator can approve or reject a submission; approved listings become publicly discoverable, and rejected listings are not public.
  6. Users cannot create, edit, or moderate listings beyond their permissions, even by calling the API directly.
  7. All v1 interface/system copy is English, while user-entered listing text remains as authored.
  8. app/, api/, and mobile/ each operate as independent Git repositories, and this SPEC.md remains in the root.

11 Decisions to settle during implementation

The product scope does not yet specify exact contact methods, township catalogs, required photo limits/storage provider, production hosting/database, account recovery, or moderation policy details. Choose simple implementations for v1, document any change to user-visible behavior in the Markdown source, and do not add payment or currency-conversion flows without a revised product decision.
